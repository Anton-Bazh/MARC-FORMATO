# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers and people of every background, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

To report a problem privately, open a minimal issue asking for a contact channel, without details; the maintainers will reach out.
